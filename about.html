About EchoArt

EchoArt was created to give students a professional, welcoming space to share creative work and support one another.

Mission

Give students an inclusive platform to share artwork, discover ideas, and inspire one another.

Vision

Grow into a creative network that can eventually connect students across multiple schools.

Values

Creativity, kindness, originality, collaboration, respect, and support for student voices.

History

EchoArt begins as a student-led idea and grows with every submission, project, and event.

Future Events

1. Featured Artwork of the Week: Each week, we'll highlight a submitted piece on our website's homepage. 2. Monthly Creative Prompts: We'll introduce monthly prompts to inspire new ideas. 3. Winter & Spring Art Events: We hope to highlight a piece that creates a meaningful emotional connection with its audience. The selected artwork will be featured on two custom mugs/T-shirts —one for the artist and one for a randomly selected staff member. We'll also create several mugs/T-shirts featuring other submitted pieces to share with staff members.
